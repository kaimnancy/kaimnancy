## Q3: How does the family know when a vaccination is due?

| Respondent | Original Response | English Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Man A | "Pichla tika kab hua tha usi ke hisab se tikakaran ka samay pata chalta hai." | "The time for the next vaccination is known based on when the previous vaccine was given." |
| Man B | "Pashu ka tikakaran ka chart banaya jata hai... us chart se pata lagta hai ki kab hoga." | "A vaccination chart is made for the animal... we find out from that chart when it will happen." |
| Man C | "Wo bata ke jate hain jo pehle lagate hain... barish ke season mein gal-ghotu vagaira (ka lagta hai)." | "The ones who administer it tell us... vaccinations like FMD (gal-ghotu) are given during the rainy season." |
| Man D | "Vaccine lagane to koi aata hi na yahan Delhi mein abhi... tabiyat kharab ho jaye to doctor vaccine/dawai de dete hain." | "No one comes to administer vaccines here in Delhi right now... if they fall ill, the doctor gives the vaccine/medicine." |
| Man E | "Hai kuch na aata aisa paas... hum to bimar hote hain tabhi dawai dete hain." | "Nothing like that comes near us... we only give medicine when they fall sick." |
| Woman A | null | null |
| Woman B | "Tikke wgaira hum nai lagwate gaon mein... bimar hoti hai bhains jab lagwate hain tikke waise nahi lagwate." | "We do not get vaccines administered in the village... we get injections given when the buffalo is sick, not otherwise." |

## Q5: What does the family lose when an animal misses a breeding cycle?

| Respondent | Original Response | English Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Man A | "Yadi pashu ka breeding cycle ruk jata hai to kisan ka nuksan hota hai." | "If the animal's breeding cycle stops, the farmer suffers a financial loss." |
| Man B | null | null |
| Man C | "Bimar hogi to doodh ka nuksan hota hai... jab tak doodh kam degi, fir wo koi aur bacha degi to wo bada hoke kaam aata hai." | "If sick, there is a loss of milk... milk yield drops, and we have to wait for it to deliver another calf to be productive again." |
| Man D | "Nuksan hi hota hai... bechni padti hai aur fir agar aage umeed nahi hoti to bech dete hain." | "It is a loss... we have to sell them, and if there is no hope (of breeding further), we just sell them." |
| Man E | "Bolne lagegi, ringne lag jae tab doctor pe tikka dharwawe (AI). Nai hoti to fir se dharwawe... doodh ka nuksan hota hai." | "She starts moaning and calling out, then we get the doctor to administer the injection (Artificial Insemination). If it doesn't work, we get it done again... there is a loss of milk." |
| Woman A | null | null |
| Woman B | "Doodh dena band kar deti hai... saal ya 10-12 mahine tak." | "She stops giving milk entirely... for a year or 10-12 months." |

## Q8: What feature or help would they wish from an AI that can assist them?

| Respondent | Original Response | English Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Man A | null | null |
| Man B | "AI se pashu ke swasth ke bare mein jankari aur lambe time tak doodh dene ke bare mein jankari (chahiye)." | "(We want) information from AI about the animal's health and information on how to ensure it gives milk for a long time." |
| Man C | "AI ye computer wale... unse pooch lo ki kab aane wali hai (vaccine)... dawai kyun khilayenge unke batane se... na doctor se hi jyada baat theek lagti hai." | "These AI/computer folks... we can ask them when the vaccine is coming... but why would we feed medicine based on what they say? No, trusting the doctor seems much better." |
| Man D | "Voice message kar denge (vaccine aur jankari ke liye) to wo theek rahega." | "If you send a voice message (for vaccines and information), that would be fine." |
| Man E | "Na, itna kada dhyan na... hum to seedha doctor ko hi bulate hain aur wahi theek hai." | "No, we don't pay that much attention (to tech)... we directly call the doctor and that is fine." |
| Woman A | "AI se pashuo ki dekhbhal hoti hai... pashuo ka uthna, bethna, chalna, khana, pina, isse humko har cheez ki madad milti hai." | "AI helps care for animals... monitoring their standing, sitting, walking, eating, drinking; we get help with everything from this." |
| Woman B | "Ye jo baat batao ho ye AI aale... ye badhe buddho te sab seekh rakhe hain. Hum in pe jyada vishwas nahi karte... hum apne hisab se apne pashuo ki dekhbhal karein hain." | "These AI folks you are talking about... we have learned all this from our elders. We do not trust them much... we take care of our animals our own way." |
